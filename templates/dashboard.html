Delay Risk Predictor Academic / Historical

Enter road-segment conditions and get a predicted delay risk class from the trained Random Forest model. All values must come from actual observed conditions; do not fabricate inputs.

Connection to Route Intelligence
Low Delay Risk
Historical route score: 0.00 – 0.35. Historically free-flowing segments with low congestion pressure and high average speed.
Medium Delay Risk
Historical route score: 0.35 – 0.65. Moderate congestion; travel time moderately impacted.
High Delay Risk
Historical route score: 0.65 – 1.00. Gridlock/heavy congestion. Emergency response historically delayed on these segments.
Academic Note:
The delay_risk target is DERIVED from the VANET congestion_label column (Gridlock/Heavy→High, Moderate→Medium, Free-Flow/Light→Low) with avg_wait_time_s fallback thresholds. It is not a ground-truth observation. This model is for academic/educational demonstration of Random Forest classification in a Data Warehouse context.

Driver Emergency Response Guidelines

• Destination Readiness: Verify triage priority (Red / Yellow / Green) and destination emergency bed capacity before transit.

• Model Selection: XGBoost Regressor provides the highest empirical predictive accuracy for complex urban arterial delays.

• Peak Traffic Hours: Exercise elevated caution on high-risk corridors during morning rush (08:00–10:00) and evening rush (17:00–20:00).

Security & Access Controls

Role-Based Access Controls:

• Administrator Role: Full analytical access to all 7 dataset visualization pages, OLAP cube operations, star schema warehouse diagnostics, and system reporting.

• Ambulance Driver Role: Streamlined access restricted exclusively to the Route Simulator and ML Delay Prediction decision support tools.

• Data Storage: PostgreSQL Star Schema hosted on Supabase Cloud with RESTful Flask telemetry middleware.

• Session Verification: Cryptographically signed HTTP-only server sessions with role-enforced endpoint barriers (HTTP 401 & 403).
