🔮 Delay Risk Predictor Academic / Historical

Enter road-segment conditions and get a predicted delay risk class from the trained Random Forest model. All values must come from actual observed conditions; do not fabricate inputs.

🛣️ Connection to Route Intelligence
Low Delay Risk
Historical route score: 0.00 – 0.35. Historically free-flowing segments with low congestion pressure and high average speed.
Medium Delay Risk
Historical route score: 0.35 – 0.65. Moderate congestion; travel time moderately impacted.
High Delay Risk
Historical route score: 0.65 – 1.00. Gridlock/heavy congestion. Emergency response historically delayed on these segments.
Academic Note:
The delay_risk target is DERIVED from the VANET congestion_label column (Gridlock/Heavy→High, Moderate→Medium, Free-Flow/Light→Low) with avg_wait_time_s fallback thresholds. It is not a ground-truth observation. This model is for academic/educational demonstration of Random Forest classification in a Data Warehouse context. Do not use for real-time emergency dispatching.
